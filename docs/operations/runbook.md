## Incident Response

1. Confirm impact and scope.
2. Protect users, data, and credentials first.
3. Contain the issue using the least risky reversible action.
4. Preserve sanitized evidence.
5. Communicate status through approved channels.
6. Recover service and validate critical workflows.
7. Record a concise incident in `.ai/memory/incidents/` when impact is significant.
